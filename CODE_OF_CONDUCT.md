# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Glassio project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful environment for everyone who participates, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and differing technical opinions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect other people's privacy.
- Share only content and code you are permitted to share.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam or other conduct that makes participation hostile or unsafe.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this code and may warn, temporarily restrict, or exclude participants depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers privately through the contact options available on the GitHub repository profile. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed channels.
